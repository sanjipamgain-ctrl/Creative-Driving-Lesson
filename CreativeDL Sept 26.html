Test day

How your test day package works.

Test day nerves are hard enough without also worrying about a car. Here's exactly what happens — the full package runs a minimum of 3 hours, covering pickup, the warm-up drive, and time at the test centre.

    We pick you up earlyOne hour before your test slot, so there's no rush getting there.

  • 03

FAQ

Good to know.

Is pickup and drop-off really included?
Yes — every lesson and every test package includes pickup at the start and drop-off at the end, at no extra cost, anywhere across Corrimal, Warrawong, Kiama and the suburbs in between.
Can I actually take my test in your car?
Yes. The car is dual-control, fully registered and insured to sit your driving test at Corrimal, Warrawong or Kiama Service NSW.
Which Service NSW centre will I test at?
Whichever is closest to you — Corrimal, Warrawong or Kiama. Tell us your suburb when you book and we'll confirm the right one.
Will I always have the same instructor?
Yes. Sanjip teaches every lesson himself, from your first booking through to test day.
How do I pay for lessons?
Book online and pay at the same time to lock in your slot. If you have any trouble, call or message Sanjip on 0435 580 102.
